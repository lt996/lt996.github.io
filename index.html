How I design

Start from the experience a feature has to deliver — calm, presence, fairness, coordination — and work backwards to the rules that guarantee it.

What I own

Features end to end: core rules, system structure, UX flows, and specs with acceptance criteria — handed to engineering, then checked against spec before launch.

How I validate

Against the spec before launch, and against what players say in the community after it. I state plainly what I would and wouldn't claim.

A real-time companion space where he keeps his own 24-hour schedule, falls asleep on his own, and is allowed to say no. Presence designed before interaction — and a daily pit stop turned into a place players linger.

Case 02 · Social System

Qimen Formation

奇门遁甲

A whole server plays one game of five-in-a-row. Players are drawn into two factions, clear challenges alone, and claim nodes together on a single shared board — with three rotating commanders per side who can point but not shout. Coordination for tens of thousands, without chat.

Case 03 · Creation System

Wishes Across the Cosmos

愿载寰宇

A Valentine's event that made the player the author of the romantic gesture: gather star sand across 12 hand-built levels with an AI companion at your side, craft a one-of-a-kind wish bottle, be witnessed — never graded — and give it away. Players took their bottles to social media on their own.
